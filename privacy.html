Privacy Policy

Rex's Rooter Plumbing Inc. · Effective November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Rex's Rooter Plumbing Inc. ("Rex's Rooter," "we," "us") collects when you visit this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies only to this website.

Information you give us

This website has no forms, accounts or online payments. The only way to contact us through the site is by tapping a button that calls (805) 929-2278. When you call, we receive the information you choose to share on the phone, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of your plumbing problem. We use it only to answer your questions, schedule and perform service, send estimates and invoices, and keep normal business records.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our web host automatically records basic technical data when pages load, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the page you visited and the time of your visit. This is used to deliver and secure the site.

Cookies and advertising

We may advertise with Google Ads and may use Google tools such as Google Ads conversion tracking and Google Analytics. When these are active, Google may place cookies or use similar technologies in your browser to:

  • measure how many people visit the site and which pages they view;
  • tell us whether a visit came from one of our ads and whether it led to a phone call (for example, which ad was clicked and the length of the call; we do not receive recordings); and
  • show our ads to people who have visited our site before, on Google and partner websites (remarketing).

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your choices:

  • Turn off personalized ads from Google at My Ad Center.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Opt out of interest-based ads from many companies at aboutads.info or networkadvertising.org.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works fully without cookies.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. There is no common industry standard for responding to it, so this site does not currently change its behavior when it receives one. You can use the choices above to limit tracking.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only as needed to run our business: with service providers such as our website host and advertising and analytics providers described above, when required by law, or to protect our rights and safety.

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may ask what personal information we hold about you from your service calls and ask us to correct or delete it, subject to legal record-keeping requirements. Call us at (805) 929-2278 to make a request. We will not discriminate against you for making a request.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we keep, and we retain customer records only as long as needed for service, warranty, tax and legal purposes.

Age requirement

This website is intended for adults 18 and older who are arranging plumbing service. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from, or sell or share the personal information of, anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has given us personal information, call us at (805) 929-2278 and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, such as Google. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
